.. A new scriv changelog fragment.

Added
-----

- Successful ``POST /auth/create-account`` responses now include
  ``next_action``: ``verify_email`` when the emailed link must be followed
  before signing in, ``sign_in`` when the new account can sign in at once.
  #4576

Fixed
-----

- When signup does not require email verification (for example
  ``AUTH_AUTOVERIFY=true`` in simple mode or
  ``AUTH_VERIFY_ACCOUNT_ENABLED=false`` in full mode), new accounts now go to
  sign-in instead of a "Check your email" page for an email that is never
  sent. A selected plan and internal redirect carry through sign-in. #4576
