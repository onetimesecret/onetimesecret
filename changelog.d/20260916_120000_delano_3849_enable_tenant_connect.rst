Added
-----

- SSO Connect is now available on custom domains. A signed-in member can attach
  the domain's SSO identity to their existing account from Connected Identities
  after re-authenticating on that domain; the panel shows Connect for the
  tenant provider. Binding requires an active organization membership for that
  exact domain. Enablement is an internal switch with no operator setting.
  (#3849, #4427)
